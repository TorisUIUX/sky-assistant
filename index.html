Sky

Sky is a personal, self-hosted voice assistant for one household. It runs on the developer's own home server and is used only by its developer. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Answers spoken questions and controls music and a shopping list at home.
  • With the developer's permission, reads (read-only) the developer's own Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail to answer questions such as "what is on my calendar tomorrow".

Google data

Sky accesses Google account data only for the developer's own account, only to answer the developer's own requests, and does not share, sell or publish it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
